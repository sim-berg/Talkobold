Welcome to Talkobold

Be heard in voice chats

Type a message and Talkobold speaks it into a virtual microphone. Choose "Talkobold Mic" as your mic in Discord, Zoom, or games, and the people you're talking to hear it — and you can hear yourself too.

Three quick steps and you're set.

Connect a voice

Talkobold uses ElevenLabs to generate speech. Paste your API key, then load your voices.

No key yet? It's free — sign up at elevenlabs.io, then Profile → API key. You can also do this later in Settings.
